BB Talkin’ Radios

Why it matters that your wingfoil instructor uses a radio headset

Wingfoil is a unique water sport. What sets it apart is the feeling of absolute flight. At our center, the height of that “flight” is no more than 85 cm — the length of the hydrofoil mast.

The magic begins when the board rises above the water and seems to float over the surface. The moment the board lifts onto the foil creates a feeling of weightlessness and flight — an emotion that is difficult to put into words.

At the same time, many students become excited or nervous at this critical moment and lose control. The student either falls into the water or the board touches down, and they have to build speed and lift onto the foil again.

01

Helping a student manage that excitement and avoid mistakes at the critical moment is the instructor’s job. This is where BB Talkin’ radios become indispensable.

02

Clear communication is the key to success

Wind noise, water and the distance between student and instructor often make normal communication difficult. BB Talkin’ radios solve this problem. A headset with an earpiece provides clear sound even in the windiest conditions.

This allows the instructor to correct mistakes in real time, explain technique and tell the student exactly what to do next.

Keep your focus on controlling the board and the wing. There is no need to turn around or struggle to hear the instructor.

03

Guidance at the right moment

Wingfoiling is built on two things: controlling the balance of the board — “pressure on the nose”, “pressure back” — and controlling the pull of the wing. Learning to maintain balance on the board is one of the hardest tasks for a beginner.

Theory alone is not enough; the student also needs to react instantly to the instructor’s advice. When guidance arrives over the radio at exactly the right time, the student understands how to react to changes in wind, water and body position. This feedback reduces mistakes and speeds up progress.

Instant guidance means no wasted time and no lost balance. Every minute of the lesson is used effectively.

04

Two-way communication makes learning more effective

BB Talkin’ radios do more than transmit instructions: they also let the student ask questions. This is especially important for beginners. If something is unclear, the student can ask immediately and receive an answer without interrupting the session.

The instructor can not only watch the student but also understand how they are feeling. This kind of communication makes every lesson more personal and effective.

05

Psychological support

Wingfoil requires concentration, but it can also cause fear or uncertainty, especially during the first sessions. A calm, supportive voice from the instructor helps the student relax — and relaxation is one of the keys to successful learning.

When students feel confident and comfortable, they learn the technique faster.

06

Conclusion

BB Talkin’ radios are more than a means of communication. They are a tool that significantly improves the quality of wingfoil training by providing clear sound, timely feedback and psychological support.

Whatever your current level, this technology helps you learn faster and get more from every session.

If you want to experience true freedom and flight over the water, make sure you have a clear connection with your instructor. BB Talkin’ is a reliable companion in the world of wingfoil.
